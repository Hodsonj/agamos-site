About Agamos

Ancient wisdom, modern romance. A quasi-reality dating show where the culture is the point, not the backdrop.

Why we made it

Every Greek-American has heard it: “So, when are you getting married?” Agamos takes that question seriously and has fun with it. We get Greek-American young adults in the same room, on camera, and let things unfold the way they actually do in our community: loud, warm, and with opinions coming from every direction.

Agamos does three things at once: entertainment, cultural connection, and matchmaking. It's a Bachelor-style quasi-reality show, it's a way for Greek-Americans to meet each other, and it's meant to end in real relationships. The old wisdom and the new romance don't always agree, and that's the show.

Agamos began as an Instagram series and grew into a full eight-episode season on YouTube, shot on a small budget with a lot of help from friends, family, and the community.
