About Agamos

The people behind Agamos.

Agamos is a Greek-American dating show built on three things: entertainment, cultural connection, and matchmaking. It is produced by a small team, with a new format every season.

JH

Joe Hodson

Producer · Editor

Joe is a data scientist by trade, and the rest of his time goes to making things, preferably things he hasn't made before. He likes the whole process of getting from an idea to a finished project. He has been filming and editing since he was a kid, and producing Agamos is the most ambitious one yet.
