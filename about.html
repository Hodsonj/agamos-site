About Agamos

Ancient wisdom, modern romance. A dating series where the culture is the point, not the backdrop.

Why we made it

Every Greek-American has heard it: “So, when are you getting married?” Agamos takes that question seriously and has fun with it. We put one bachelorette in front of six Greek-American men and let the dates unfold the way they actually do in our community: loud, warm, and with opinions coming from every direction.

The show is about more than matchmaking. It's about what it means to date inside a culture that prizes family, faith, and tradition while living a fully modern American life. The old wisdom and the new romance don't always agree, and that's the show.

Agamos began as an Instagram series and grew into a full eight-episode season on YouTube, shot on a small budget with a lot of help from friends, family, and the community.

How a season works

First dates. The bachelorette meets each of the six men one on one. Two episodes, three dates each.

Second dates. Everyone gets a second look. The stakes go up and so do the questions.

The finale. A three-part finale where she narrows it down, the families get involved, and a decision gets made.

Season 1's bachelors: George, Anthony, Vasilios, Adoni, Stephen, and James. Their dates and the finale are all on the episodes list.
