ἄγαμος · agamos · unmarried.

Agamos is a dating series made by and for the Greek-American community. A single bachelorette meets six eligible Greek-American men, and the whole thing plays out the way it does in real life: over food, in front of family, with everyone's opinion in the room.

It started as an Instagram show and grew into a full eight-episode season on YouTube. Season 2 is casting now.
